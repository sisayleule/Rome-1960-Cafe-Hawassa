Discover Rome 1960

Rome 1960 Cafe combines fast-food favorites with authentic Ethiopian coffee culture. From crispy fried chicken and gourmet pizzas to freshly brewed espresso and traditional macchiatos, we offer a warm, welcoming dining experience in the heart of Hawassa.

Whether you're here for a quick coffee, a family meal, or a night out with friends, our modern space and friendly staff are ready to make your visit memorable.

About Rome 1960

Our story, our passion, our commitment to excellence

Our Story

Rome 1960 was created with a simple vision: to bring together the best of fast-food dining and authentic coffee culture in one welcoming space. Located in the heart of Hawassa at Piazza, we've built a place where quality food meets warm Ethiopian hospitality.

Every dish is prepared fresh, every cup is brewed with care, and every guest is treated like family. That's the Rome 1960 promise.
