Our Story

Rome 1960 was created with a simple vision: to bring together the best of fast-food dining and authentic coffee culture in one welcoming space. Located in the heart of Hawassa at Piazza, we've built a place where quality food meets warm Ethiopian hospitality.

Every dish is prepared fresh, every cup is brewed with care, and every guest is treated like family. That's the Rome 1960 promise.

Our Philosophy

We believe great food and great coffee can bring people together. Our philosophy is simple: use quality ingredients, serve with hospitality, and create a space where everyone feels welcome. From our Ethiopian espresso to our signature pizzas, every item reflects our commitment to excellence.

Our Values

Quality

We use only the finest ingredients and maintain the highest standards in everything we serve.

Hospitality

Warm and welcoming service that makes every guest feel at home, just like Ethiopian tradition.

Community

A gathering place for families, students, and friends in the heart of Hawassa.
